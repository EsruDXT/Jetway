F.A.Q

Having troubles? We are here to help.

How do I book a ticket? ∨
You can book a ticket through our website or mobile app. Simply search for your destination, select your flight, and complete the payment process.
How can I buy insurance for my flight? ∨
Flight insurance can be purchased during the booking process or by contacting our customer service team.
How do I change my account password? ∨
Go to your account settings, select "Change Password", enter your current password and new password, then confirm the changes.
Can I cancel my ticket reservation? ∨
Yes, you can cancel your reservation through your account. Cancellation fees may apply depending on the ticket type and timing.
Why can't I make a reservation? ∨
This could be due to payment issues, unavailable flights, or technical problems. Please contact our support team for assistance.
How do I change my personal informations? ∨
Log in to your account, go to profile settings, and update your personal information. Make sure to save your changes.
